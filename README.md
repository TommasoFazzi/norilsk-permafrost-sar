Question: is the ground under Norilsk moving in a widespread way because of
thaw (summers 2017–2026)? Three hypotheses, each with an observable signature:

| Hypothesis | Expected ground behaviour |
|---|---|
| Climatic thaw | Subsidence also in natural tundra far from structures; seasonal cycle + multi-year trend tied to temperature |
| Anthropogenic thaw | Subsidence concentrated around structures / heat sources; nearby tundra stable |
| Structural defect / corrosion | Ground stable (consistent with, not proof of) |

Design: industrial area vs natural tundra controls with similar elevation
and slope (not floodplain, not wetland); InSAR referenced to bedrock on the
Kharaelakh plateau NE of Talnakh, away from mines.

### Hypothesis test (what is actually measured)

Snow decorrelates C-band, so each summer is a separate InSAR stack, and
Sentinel-1 has **no acquisitions at all over Norilsk in 2022-2024**. A direct
multi-year subsidence trend is therefore **not observable**. The test is:

> **seasonal subsidence vs sqrt(cumulative TDD), per summer, per zone**
> (industrial / urban terraces vs tundra control), referenced to plateau rock.

- Climatic thaw: tundra and urban both follow sqrt(TDD); the slope (a proxy
  for active-layer ice content / thaw depth) grows across summers.
- Anthropogenic thaw: urban/industrial slope clearly larger than tundra in
  the same summer; tundra stable or small.
- Structural defect: no significant seasonal subsidence in either zone.
Annual (late-summer to late-summer) pairs are an optional extra, coherent only
on built-up and rock.

### TPP-3 tank time series (all seasons, 20 m, one burst)

Burst `136_290630_IW2` contains TPP-3 and the tank-5 estimate
(`tank5_zhang2022`, 1.9 km inside the burst edge). 87 acquisitions
2017-06-19 .. 2020-10-13, winters included; last one before the collapse
2020-05-10. Single burst at 5x1 looks = 1 credit/pair; products are cropped to
+-6 km around the tank on download (`--crop-km`), so they stay small.
**Use `--no-water-mask`**: the HyP3 water mask covers the tank row next to the
Daldykan river and zeroes the phase there (job `norilsk_tank`, kept, has it on).

```bash
python 01_s1_search.py --burst 136_290630_IW2 --window 2017-06-01 2020-10-15 --out s1_bursts_tank.csv
O="--path 136 --bursts s1_bursts_tank.csv --name norilsk_tank_nowm --looks 5x1 --continuous --n-next 3 --years 2017 2018 2019 2020"
python 02_s1_hyp3.py plan $O                  # 255 pairs, 255 credits
python 02_s1_hyp3.py submit $O --no-water-mask
python 02_s1_hyp3.py status --path 136 --name norilsk_tank_nowm
python 02_s1_hyp3.py download --path 136 --name norilsk_tank_nowm --crop-km 6   # -> data/hyp3/norilsk_tank_nowm/
python 02_s1_hyp3.py download --path 136 --name norilsk_tank_nowm --out norilsk_tank_wide \
    --crop-km 8 --crop-point burst_tanks_centre   # same jobs, wider crop with the depot tanks (step 19)
# HyP3 keeps products ~14 days after processing: download both crops in time.
python 09_timeseries.py --conncomp reference --years 2017 2018 2019 2020 2021 2025 2026
python 18_tank_series.py      # -> outputs/tank_series.json, tank_series.csv, fig_tank_series.png
```

Step 18 asks whether the tank moved relative to the structures around it
before the collapse more than comparable targets do (damage comes from
differential settlement). Tank candidates: the separate amplitude-loss maxima
between the snow-free summers 2019 and 2020 within 300 m of the published
point (a destroyed shell stops reflecting). For each candidate and the
published point: 3x3 window minus the winter-coherent targets 0.3-1.5 km
away (same SNAPHU component), SBAS on the pairs before the collapse. The
tank decorrelates at snowmelt and snow onset, so the series falls into
separate segments (no multi-year trend): the change over each segment is
compared with Zhang's rate (−35 mm/yr) and, like the snow-free summer
changes and the relative winter coherence, with 200 bright targets
1.5-5.5 km away with the same winter coherence. Plus the TPP-3 polygon and
tank window on the 40 m summer stacks with the spatial-shift null. Point
targets hop by one pixel between products (each pair is coregistered to
its own reference date), so amplitude and coherence maps are 3x3 means.
Result: RESULTS.md, section "TPP-3 tank no. 5".

## v1 robustness checks

`09_timeseries.py --conncomp reference` keeps only phase that SNAPHU
unwrapped in the same connected component as the plateau reference. In the
June pairs (snow on the plateau) the lowland is a separate component, so its
phase relative to the reference is off by an unknown whole number of cycles
(2.8 cm LOS); v0 (`--conncomp any`, default, kept for reproducibility) mixes
those pairs in. Use `reference` for anything compared with the reference or
between distant zones.

## Before analysis

- `config.py`: the HPP-3 tank location differs between sources (two points
  kept, unresolved; see `tank_amplitude.py`). Best estimate: `tank5_zhang2022`
  (Zhang et al. 2022, Remote Sens. 14, 5036), 0.35 km from the TPP-3 polygon;
  `hpp3_tank_b` is the generic Wikipedia coordinate, 9 km away. `tundra_control` is still a
  placeholder: choose it from `tundra_candidate_v2.tif`.
- `plateau_reference` sits at the east edge of BBOX: most of the plateau is
  outside the AOI. Extending BBOX east (~88.9 E) is likely free in bursts.
- Mask v1 (step 05) put the reference on the mining massif south of Norilsk
  and WorldCover maps most industry as "bare": use the v2 masks.
- ERA5-Land soil temperature (1-2.5 m) is positive in continuous permafrost:
  **not usable** as evidence of ground warming. Use CCI (step 07).
  Open-Meteo `era5_land` has no snowfall; snow depth is used instead.
- HyP3 free quota: 8,000 credits/month; multi-burst 10x2 with 4-9 bursts =
  5 credits/pair. All 7 summers with data: 99 pairs ≈ 495 credits.
- Sentinel-1 path 136 DESC is the only usable stack (no ascending). Frame
  numbers drift (360 vs 358+363), hence burst processing. **No S1 data at
  all in 2022-2024** (S1B failed Dec 2021; S1A did not cover Norilsk);
  2025 = S1C, 2026 = S1C/D.
- NISAR PROVISIONAL products start 2026-06-17 → one summer, no trend. Two
  tracks cover the AOI fully: 111 ASC (frame 037) and 6 DESC (frame 053),
  so 2026 allows an ascending/descending (vertical vs east-west) check.
- HyP3 `conncomp`: component 0 = not unwrapped; other labels are separate
  unwrapping regions. Only the reference's component is tied to the plateau
  (see v1 robustness checks).

## Evidence strength (state it in the submission)

1. Air warming (ERA5): strong. 2. Ground warming (CCI): medium, modelled,
   ~1 km, ends 2023 (ERA5-Land soil temperature not usable).
3. Ice-rich ground under structures: weak, low-res maps only.
4. Surface deformation (InSAR): strong, summer only, area-wide.
5. Differential settlement of a single tank: medium. The 20 m all-season
   series (step 18) measures the tank relative to nearby structures in
   separate segments (gaps at snowmelt / snow onset), one track, one sensor;
   it cannot resolve tilt within one tank (~45 m across, 2-3 pixels).
